Payment Help

We support two ways to pay, depending on where you're paying from.

Razorpay (India)

Pay by card, UPI, netbanking, or wallet through Razorpay's secure checkout. Payment is verified automatically — your download link appears within seconds of a successful payment.

Wise (International)

You'll be taken to a secure Wise payment page. Because Wise confirmation currently requires a short manual check on our end, please send us your Order ID (shown after you pay) through our Contact page so we can confirm and send your link — usually within a few hours.

My payment succeeded but I didn't get a link

This is rare, but if it happens: for Razorpay, refresh the product page and check your email; if nothing arrives within 15 minutes, contact us with your payment reference. For Wise, see above — a short manual step is expected.

Was I charged twice?

Contact us immediately via our Contact page with both transaction references. See our Refund Policy for how duplicate charges are handled.

Is my payment information safe?

Yes. We never see or store your card, UPI, or bank details — these are handled entirely by Razorpay and Wise on their own secure systems.
