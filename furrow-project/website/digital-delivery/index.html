Digital Delivery

Every product on Furrow is a digital file — there is no physical shipping, ever.

How delivery works

Once your payment is verified, a unique, one-time download link is generated automatically and shown on screen immediately. For Razorpay payments, this happens within seconds. For Wise payments, it follows once we've confirmed your payment, usually within a few hours.

About your download link

Your link is personal to your order and works once. It also expires after a set time window (typically 15–60 minutes) whether or not it's been used. This protects the book from unauthorized sharing — see our Download Help page if your link has expired or stopped working.

File format

Books are delivered as PDF files. Some products (like templates) may include additional formats — check each product's page for details.

Need your link resent?

Visit Download Help to request a reissue, or reach us via our Contact page.
